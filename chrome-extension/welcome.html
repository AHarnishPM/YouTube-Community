This extension shows timestamped YouTube comments over the video right when they come up. To do that, it sends the ID of each YouTube video you watch to our server, which looks up that video's public comments, captions, and chapters using YouTube API Services and stores them for up to 29 days. It never accesses your Google account and doesn't store anything that identifies you.

By turning it on, you agree to our privacy policy and to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

You're all set. Open any YouTube video to see comments appear. You can turn the extension off in its popup.
