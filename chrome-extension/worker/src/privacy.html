YouTube Community privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

YouTube Community is a free browser extension that shows timestamped YouTube comments over the video at the moment they refer to. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the extension accesses

  • The ID of the YouTube video you're watching, so it can look up that video's comments.
  • Public information about that video: its length, captions, chapters, and public top-level comments (commenter display name, comment text, like count, reply count, and posting time).
  • Your settings (comment size and how many comments to show), stored only in your browser.

The extension does not access your Google or YouTube account, your watch history, or any personal information about you, and it never posts anything on your behalf.

What is stored, and for how long

The public video information above is stored in our database, keyed by video ID, so it can be shared with other users of the extension instead of being fetched from YouTube again. Comments are refreshed every few hours while people watch a video, and anything not refreshed for 29 days is automatically deleted. Nothing in the database identifies who watched which video.

Our server is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver requests and to limit abuse. We don't store IP addresses.

Sharing

We don't sell or share data with anyone. Stored data is shown only to users of the extension.

Your choices

You can turn the extension off at any time from its popup, which stops it from contacting our server, or remove it from your browser. You can also revoke this extension's access to your data via the Google security settings page, although the extension never connects to your Google account. To ask us to delete stored data (for example, a comment of yours), email aaronjharnish@gmail.com; we'll delete it within 7 days.
